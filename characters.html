Characters

Characters

Chihaya Ayase

A determined high school girl who was inspired by Arata in elementary school to play karuta and to dream of becoming the "Queen" of karuta. She begins a karuta club at her high school with Taichi.

She is first introduced to competitive karuta by her childhood friend, Arata. Karuta has remained as Chihaya's passion despite being separated from Arata. She has an exceptional hearing ability that gives her an advantage in playing karuta.

Taichi Mashima

Chihaya's childhood best friend. Taichi Mashima is an intelligent boy from a rich family who is very popular amongst the kids at their elementary school. He is almost instantly good at everything he does and is referred to in the series as a “Jack of all trades, master of none”.

He considers giving up karuta early on in the series after becoming convinced that he could spend his entire life on the game without ever becoming better than Arata Wataya. His confidence builds, however, with his continued participation in the world of karuta, and it is shown that it is his own perception of his skill in karuta and his weak self-confidence that is holding him back.

Fun Fact: Has exceptionally terrible luck in Karuta, often ending up in a battle of fates, otherwise known as unmei-san.

Arata Wataya

Arata Wataya is a student from Fukui who once transferred to Chihaya Ayase's elementary school as a child. The grandson of a master karuta player, Arata inspired Chihaya to take up karuta by telling her that he thinks she has a talent for it. He had difficulty fitting in because of his Fukui dialect and his family's poverty. His passion for karuta and his playing style are inspired by his grandfather, a Meijin (master).

Fun Fact: Often looks at Chihaya's sister Chitose's modeling work due to her resemblance to Chihaya.

Shinobu Wakamiya

The current Queen of Karuta, the national champion of the women's division, she is also the youngest Queen in history. When she was younger there was this girl that was a close Karuta friend, but because Shinobu was very skilled in Karuta she wasn't allowed to play Karuta with kids her age.

Due to her difficult childhood of enforced distancing from people she counts the karuta cards as her friends, even apologizing to them internally if she manages to lose them to an opponent. She also has difficulty in relating with other people her age.

Fun Fact: She adores snowmaru and other obscure merchandise. She initialy bonds with Chihaya over this strange obsession

Hisashi Suo

Hisashi Suo is the current Meijin (Master) of Karuta. He began karuta in college, and took three years to take the title of Meijin. He has won the Master title five consecutive times. He loves sweets and is known for his lazy demeanor. He mentored Taichi later in the series

Fun Fact: He loves sweets and often gives them out at competitions.
