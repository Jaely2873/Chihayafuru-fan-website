Karuta

What is Karuta?

Karuta is a card game in Japan and the competitive form is based around the Hyakunin Isshu. The cards themselves date back to around the Asako period while the game originated in the Edo period. The game is often played at New Year's celebrations. The series Chihayafuru helped bring international attention to the game.
