Karuta

What is Karuta?

Karuta is a card game in Japan and the competitive form is based around the Hyakunin Isshu. The cards themselves date back to around the Asako period while the game originated in the Edo period. The game is often played at New Year's celebrations. The series Chihayafuru helped bring international attention to the game.

Types of cards

The Hyakunin Isshu contains 100 cards. There are two types of cards within it;

Yomifuda: these cards contain the full poem, and illustration, and the author.

Torifuda: These cards only contain the last two lines of the poem

How to Play

Each player gets 25 torifuda cards and arranges them in three rows. A card reader, either a person or a recording, will read out a yomifuda card, and it is the player's job to take the matching torifuda card before their opponent does. Because there are only 50 cards on the board at max, that means there are 50 karafuda, or "dead cards." The goal of the game is to get rid of all the cards on your side of the field.

If you take a card from your territory you reduce your card count by one.

If you take a card from your opponent's territory you give them a card from your own side.

Penalties

For any penalties that you gain your opponent sends a card over.

  • Touching a card on your opponent's side when the correct card is on your side.

  • Touching a card on your side when the card is on your opponent's side

  • Touching a card when the torifuda to the corrisponding poem is not on the board, karafuda.
