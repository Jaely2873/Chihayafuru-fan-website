Welcome to the world of Chihayafuru!

Chihayafuru is a Japanese manga series by Yuki Suegetsu and was published from 2007 to 2022. It follows high schooler Chihaya Ayase, and her quest to become the queen of Karuta


Chihayafuru has amassed a large global fanbase. By February of 2025, Chihayafuru has over 29 million copies in circulation. The series has also greatly increased the popularity of Karuta within Japan.

On this website you can find descriptions for each character, a explanation on what karuta is, and a short quiz to test how well you know your favorite characters!
